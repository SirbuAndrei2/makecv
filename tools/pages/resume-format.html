Format is everything about your resume except the words: the structure, length, order of sections, file type and page setup. Good formatting is invisible. It lets a recruiter find what they need in seconds. Here are the decisions that matter, with a recommendation for each.

Which resume format: chronological, functional or hybrid?

FormatWhat it isUse it when
Reverse chronologicalJobs listed newest first, each with its achievementsAlmost always. Recruiters and ATS expect it.
Hybrid (combination)A skills or highlights section first, then a chronological work historyCareer changers whose transferable skills need explaining
FunctionalSkills grouped by theme, with little or no work historyRarely. Many recruiters read it as hiding something, and parsers struggle to attach skills to jobs.

MakeCV's templates all use the reverse chronological structure. Career changers can get a hybrid effect by writing a strong summary and leading the skills list with transferable skills.

How long should a resume be?

  • Students, graduates and up to about 5–7 years' experience: one page.
  • Experienced professionals and managers: two pages.
  • Executives: two pages, occasionally three.
  • Academic CV: as long as your publication record requires (see resume vs CV).
  • UK CV: two sides of A4 is the usual guide (see the UK CV guide).

The test isn't the page count; it is whether every line earns its place. Cut duties, old jobs and generic skills before you cut achievements.

Section order

  1. Name and contact details
  2. Professional summary
  3. Work experience (or Education first, if you are a student or recent graduate)
  4. Education
  5. Skills
  6. Optional: projects, certifications, languages, volunteering

Some templates move skills and languages to a side column. That changes where they appear, not their priority.

PDF or Word?

Follow the advert. If it asks for .docx, send .docx. Otherwise a text-based PDF is the safer default: fonts, spacing and layout look the same on every device, and modern applicant tracking systems read it well.

The PDF to avoid is an image-only one: a scan, a photo, or a design exported as a picture. In MakeCV, the ATS PDF button creates a text-based PDF through your browser's print dialog, while Download PDF creates an image-based file intended for people. Details in the ATS guide.

A4 or US Letter?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in, 216 × 279 mm) is standard in the US and Canada; A4 (210 × 297 mm) everywhere else. A4 is slightly taller and narrower. Online, nobody notices; on paper, an A4 page printed on Letter loses a little at the bottom margin. MakeCV exports A4. If you are applying in North America and expect your resume to be printed, keep the bottom of your page a little lighter.

Fonts, size and spacing

  • Font: any common, readable font. Each MakeCV template comes with its own professional font pairing, so there is nothing to choose.
  • Size: body text 10–12 pt; your name 18–28 pt; section headings 11–14 pt.
  • Margins: roughly 1.5–2.5 cm (0.6–1 in). Narrower looks cramped; wider wastes space.
  • Line spacing: 1.15–1.5 for body text, with clear space between sections.
  • Colour: one accent colour at most for headings and lines; body text in black or dark grey.

Dates and consistency

Pick one date style and use it everywhere: "Mar 2022 – Present" or "03/2022 – present". Months matter: "2021 – 2022" could mean two months or two years. MakeCV formats dates for you from the month and year you choose.

File name

Use Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf (or -CV.pdf in the UK). Avoid "resume_final_v3.pdf". When you save MakeCV's ATS PDF, the print dialog suggests a file name based on your name.
