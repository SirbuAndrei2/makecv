Frequently Asked Questions

Is this resume builder completely free?
Yes. MakeCV has no paid plan, no watermark and no account. The site is funded by ads, which never appear in the PDF you download.
Do I need to create an account to download my resume?
No sign-up needed. Fill in your details, pick a template, and click Download — your PDF resume is ready instantly, with no email required.
What format is the resume exported in?
You get two PDF options. Download PDF creates an A4 PDF that looks exactly like the preview; it is rendered as an image, so its text cannot be selected. ATS PDF opens your browser's print dialog — choose “Save as PDF” to get a text-based PDF that applicant tracking systems can read. Use the ATS PDF for online applications (how ATS reads your resume).
Is my resume data stored or shared?
No. Your resume is built in your browser and is never sent to our servers. Nothing is saved either: refreshing the page clears the form, so finish and download your PDF in one session.
Which resume templates are available?
MakeCV offers 10 templates: Classic, Modern (sidebar), Minimalist, Creative, Executive, Tech/Dark, Bloom, Neon, Aurora and Swiss. Compare all 10 templates to see which suits your industry and whether it is ATS-safe.
Will my downloaded resume have a watermark?
No. Every resume you download from MakeCV is watermark-free. The PDF is clean, with no branding overlaid on your content.
Are the resume templates ATS-friendly?
Classic, Minimalist and Executive are single-column layouts with standard section headings, the safest choice for applicant tracking systems. Whichever template you pick, download it with the ATS PDF button so the text stays machine-readable. See the ATS-friendly resume guide.
Resume or CV — which one do I need?
In the US and Canada a resume is a 1–2 page summary of your experience, while a CV is a longer academic record. In the UK, Ireland and most of Europe, “CV” is simply the name for that 1–2 page document. MakeCV builds the 1–2 page version. Resume vs CV explained.
Can I use this resume builder on my phone or tablet?
Yes. MakeCV works on desktop, tablet and mobile. On mobile you can switch between the edit view and a full live preview of your resume with a single tap.
