Professional Resume Template (Two-Column, Free)

By the MakeCV team · Updated October 27, 2026

A professional resume template should look confident without getting in the way of what you have done. MakeCV's Professional template does that with a two-column layout: a wide main column for your profile, experience, education and projects, and a light sidebar on the right for contact details, skills, languages and certifications. A square photo is optional and sits top right; skip it and the space simply closes up.

When a two-column resume works

  • You have a lot of short items. Skills, languages and certifications fit neatly in the sidebar instead of taking full-width lines, so more of your experience fits on one page.
  • A person reads it first. Recruiters, hiring managers and referrals open the PDF directly; the layout lets them find your contact details and key skills at a glance.
  • You want something more polished than a plain document but more restrained than a creative design. Navy and blue on white suits sales, operations, HR, finance and project management.

The ATS question

Applicant tracking systems convert your PDF to text before anyone reads it. Two-column layouts carry a little more risk than one column, because a parser may read across both columns. The Professional template keeps that risk low: everything with sentences (profile, experience, education, projects) is in the main column, and the sidebar holds only short labelled items.

Two files, one minute: download Professional with Download PDF for email and referrals, then switch to Harvard or Classic and save an ATS PDF for portals such as Workday or Taleo. Your details carry over, so there is nothing to retype. More in the ATS resume guide.

How to fill it in

  • Profile: two to four sentences at the top of the main column: your role, years of experience, one measurable result, and what you want next. See summary examples.
  • Experience: one achievement per line, starting with a verb. Each line becomes a bullet, and dates line up on the right.
  • Skills: six to ten, each one to three words. Set a level for each; the template shows it as one to three dots. Keep the names matched to the job advert, since that is what a parser reads.
  • Certifications: name, issuer and year. Recent and relevant ones only.

Frequently asked questions

Is a two-column resume ATS-friendly?
It can be. The Professional template keeps your experience in a wide main column and puts only short items (contact details, skills, languages, certifications) in a narrow light sidebar, which most parsers handle well. For strict portals, download the ATS PDF or switch to a single-column template such as Harvard or Classic.
What makes a resume template look professional?
Consistency more than decoration: one accent colour, aligned dates, the same date format everywhere, bullets that start with verbs, and enough white space that each section is easy to find.
Should I include a photo?
Only where it is the local norm, such as parts of continental Europe and Asia. In the US, Canada, the UK and Ireland, leave it out. The Professional template shows no photo area at all if you don't upload one.
How is Professional different from Modern?
Professional has a white header, a light grey sidebar on the right and dot-based skill levels: it prints with little ink and reads as corporate. Modern has a dark sidebar on the left with skill bars, which looks bolder on screen.
