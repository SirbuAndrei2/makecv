Tech Resume Templates for Software Engineers

By the MakeCV team · Updated October 27, 2026

Engineering resumes are skimmed by people who read code all day: they want your stack, what you shipped, and evidence it worked. MakeCV's Tech / Dark template looks like a well-themed editor, with a dark navy page, Space Grotesk type and blue accents. Neon goes further, with a near-black page, cyan and magenta highlights and skill bars.

What a software engineering resume needs

A stack you can scan in five seconds

Add your languages, frameworks, databases and infrastructure tools as skills, most relevant first. Match the job advert's spelling ("PostgreSQL", not "Postgres DB") so keyword searches find you. Eight to fifteen items is typical. More in how to choose resume skills.

Experience written as impact

Each role should answer: what did you build or change, with what, and what happened? A useful pattern is action + system + measurable result:

Example

Moved the billing service from cron jobs to an event queue (Go, SQS), cutting failed invoice runs from roughly 40 a month to 2, and on-call pages for billing by half.

If you don't have hard numbers, describe scale (requests per second, users, data volume) or the before/after.

Projects, especially early in your career

The Projects section has room for three entries with a name, the tech used and a one-line description. Students and career changers should treat this as their main experience section. See resume with no experience.

Links

Put GitHub or a personal site in the Website / Portfolio field. Only link repositories that are tidy, with a README.

Dark templates and portals. Big-tech and enterprise application systems parse your file. A dark page with a side column parses less predictably than a light single column, and some reviewers print resumes. Use Tech / Dark or Neon for direct applications and keep a Classic version for portals. Switching templates keeps everything you typed.

Downloading

The ATS PDF button keeps the dark background (it forces backgrounds on in the print output) and produces selectable text. Download PDF gives a pixel-perfect image-based copy, fine for emailing to a person. See the ATS guide for the difference.

Frequently asked questions

Is a dark resume a good idea for a software engineer?
It works when a person reads it on screen, for example a startup founder or an engineering manager you email directly. For large companies whose portals parse your file, a light single-column version (Classic) is safer. Keep both.
Should I put a GitHub link on my resume?
Yes, if it shows work you are happy for a hiring manager to read. Put it in the Website / Portfolio field so it appears in the header, and link specific repositories from the Projects section.
How do I list programming languages and tools?
Add each one as a skill ("TypeScript", "PostgreSQL", "Kubernetes"). Prioritise the ones in the job advert, and prove the important ones in your experience and projects with what you built.
