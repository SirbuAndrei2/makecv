Quick comparison

"ATS" below is our own assessment of how safely a parser can read the layout. Best means one reading column and standard headings; Good means a narrow side column with plain headings; Fair means a sidebar, cards or a dark page, which look great to a human reader but carry more parsing risk.

How to choose in 30 seconds

  1. Applying through an online portal (Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo, iCIMS and similar)? Pick Classic or Minimalist and download the ATS PDF.
  2. Senior role, conservative industry (law, finance, government)? Executive or Classic.
  3. Emailing a person directly, or a design/creative role? Any template works; Modern, Aurora, Creative and Bloom stand out most.
  4. Developer or data role? Tech / Dark reads well on screen; keep Classic as a second version for strict portals.
  5. Applying in the UK or Ireland? Skip the photo (see the UK CV guide). In parts of continental Europe a photo is still common.

Because your details are typed in once, it costs nothing to keep two versions: a design-forward PDF for people and a plain one for portals.

Frequently asked questions

Are these resume templates really free?
Yes. All 10 templates are free to use and download as PDF, with no account, no trial and no watermark. MakeCV is paid for by ads on the website, never in your PDF.
Can I download the templates as Word files?
No. MakeCV exports PDF only: a design-accurate PDF and a text-based ATS PDF. If an employer insists on a .docx file, keep your text in a document editor as well and paste it into their template.
Which template is best for applicant tracking systems?
Classic and Minimalist are single-column layouts with standard headings, so they are the safest. Executive and Swiss keep a narrow side column but still use plain headings. Whatever you choose, download it with the ATS PDF button. See the ATS resume guide.
Can I switch templates after filling in my details?
Yes. Your details are entered once and every template reads from them, so you can switch templates in step 2 or from the preview as often as you like without retyping anything.
